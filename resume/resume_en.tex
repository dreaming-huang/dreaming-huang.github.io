\section{\faUsers\ Projects}
\datedsubsection{\textbf{Physically Based Facial Texture Generation in the Wild} (TPAMI 2025)}{Apr. 2023 -- Apr. 2024}
Co-first author. A multi-stage framework for text-driven physically based facial texture generation in the wild, compatible with the PBR pipeline and free of Light Stage capture. Integrates differentiable rendering and texture-disentanglement regularization to train a GAN, aligns its latent space with text embeddings, and introduces an edge-aware Score Distillation Sampling (EASDS) boosting scheme for more diverse generation and efficient SDS optimization.
\datedsubsection{\textbf{BuildingBlock: Structured Building Generation} (SIGGRAPH 2025)}{Apr. 2024 -- Apr. 2025}
Co-first author. A hybrid approach that integrates generative models, procedural content generation (PCG), and large language models. The Layout Generation Phase recasts box-based layout generation as point-cloud generation and extends layouts into rule-based hierarchical designs with LLMs; the Building Construction Phase uses those layouts to guide PCG for locally customizable, high-quality structured buildings.
\datedsubsection{\textbf{CG-MLLM: 3D Captioning and Generation} (ICML 2026)}{Apr. 2024 -- May 2026}
A unified Multi-modal Large Language Model for 3D captioning and high-resolution 3D generation. A Mixture-of-Transformer architecture decouples token-level and block-level autoregressive modeling, combining a pre-trained vision-language backbone with a 3D VAE latent space so standard tokens and spatial blocks interact in one framework.
\datedsubsection{\textbf{National Key Project on High-Altitude Inspection}}{}
Participated in a national key project on high-altitude inspection.
